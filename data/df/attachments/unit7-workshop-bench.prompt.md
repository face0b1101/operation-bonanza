## Prompt A

Create a photorealistic photograph taken on a mobile phone inside a small British
machining workshop, for a UK law enforcement training exercise on firearms
reactivation offences. The scene is a training reconstruction, not a real crime scene.

Landscape orientation. A steel workbench under a fluorescent strip light, with a bench
vice bolted to the near edge. Laid out on a grey cloth on the bench are three
deactivated handgun frames of an older European service pattern, clearly inert: barrels
visibly blocked with a welded plug, slides removed and set to one side, no magazines,
no ammunition anywhere in frame. Beside them are engineering files, a set of drill bits,
a digital vernier caliper, a tin of cutting oil and a spiral-bound notebook with pencil
figures.

PHOTOGRAPH CHARACTERISTICS

- Handheld, taken from standing height looking down at about 45 degrees
- Harsh overhead fluorescent light, hard shadows under the tools, slight green cast
- Swarf and metal dust on the bench surface, an oily rag at the edge of frame
- Phone camera character: noise in the shadows, blown highlights on the bare metal,
  slightly soft focus at the far end of the bench
- No people in frame, no faces, no text overlay, no watermark
